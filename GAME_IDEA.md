# Crit & Cast

A turn-based tactical roguelike on a grid, with TTRPG-style rules: classes, d20 rolls, stats, and both melee and spells.

## References

- **Rift Wizard:** spell builds, readable grid combat, depth from systems
- **Batomon:** a shop phase after every battle, mail-like battles, runs that last days, a different opponent each time
- **MapleStory:** job advancement, stat points, odd builds like dexless
- **Magicraft:** spell crafting and wild spell combos
- **TTRPGs (Tormenta 20, D&D):** classes, d20 rolls, spells, attributes
- *(more to add)*

## The run

1. Click play and choose a class.
2. Fight a battle.
3. Win and level up:
   - Pick 1 power: a class power (spells for a mage, techniques for a barbarian) or a general power.
   - Spend stat points (STR, DEX, INT…) MapleStory-style.
4. Visit the shop.
5. Next battle. Repeat until you die or beat the run.

A run can be started today and finished a week later.

## Progression (MapleStory-inspired)

- **Job advancement:** at certain points in a run, the class promotes. For example, Barbarian becomes Berserker or Warlord.
- **Stat points:** the player builds the character their own way.
- **Odd builds are allowed:** things like dexless builds and broken spell combos are part of the fun.

## Classes

- **Barbarian (first):** melee fighter who hits hard and gets stronger when angry. Strike, Cleave, Leap, Rage.
- **Mage (next):** spells like Fireball, Lightning Touch, Counterspell.
- More classes later.

## PvE first, PvP later

**The core is PvE:** runs against AI enemies and bosses. In PvE, overpowered builds are a reward, not a problem. A mage build that breaks the game by level 4 is fine.

**PvP as a later mode, Batomon-style:**
- Play-by-mail battles against a **different player each time**. Because you never face the same opponent over and over, an overpowered combo feels like a fun challenge instead of an unfair one. The focus stays on your own build.
- **Counterplay** keeps it fair: Counterspell against mages, abilities that answer specific strategies.
- **Ghost builds:** you can also fight other players' saved builds, controlled by the AI.
- Live PvP over WebSockets is a maybe for much later.

## Notes

- Animations: start with simple generic effects (hit flash, damage numbers, projectile, burst) and make cool custom ones in the polish phase.
- Start small: one Barbarian against a few enemies on a small grid.
